[03 — Process]

Yarn in. Garment out.

  1. [Step 1]

    Knitting

    Cotton yarn is knitted into fabric — jersey, rib, interlock, fleece or terry.

  2. [Step 2]

    Dyeing

    Greige fabric is dyed and processed to the shade you approve.

  3. [Step 3]

    Cutting

    Fabric is laid and cut into panels to your pattern.

  4. [Step 4]

    Stitching

    Panels are sewn into the finished style.

  5. [Step 5]

    Finishing

    Garments are checked, pressed, labelled and packed.
